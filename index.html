Matteo Contini

Researcher in marine ecosystem monitoring and AI

Recently completed a PhD focused on multi-scale mapping of changes in tropical reefs at IFREMER (DOI) and INRIA (IROKO Pl@ntNet team), based in La Réunion. I am particularly interested in applying artificial intelligence to environmental monitoring, biodiversity conservation and large-scale ecosystem analysis. I welcome collaborations and research opportunities in these domains.

More information about my projects can be found on the SEATIZEN monitoring website: seatizenmonitoring.ifremer.re.
